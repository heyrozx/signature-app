## Full description (max 4000)

Simple Signature Maker turns your finger into a pen. Sign on a clean white
canvas, then save or share your signature as an image you can drop into
documents, contracts, forms and chats.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Clean canvas – nothing on screen but your signature
• Smooth strokes – lines are rounded into natural curves as you draw
• Undo & redo – fix a stroke without starting over; even "clear" can be undone
• Six ink colors – black, red, green, blue, yellow and brown; each stroke
  keeps its own color
• Pen size and tip – adjust the thickness in 1 px steps and choose a round,
  flat or square tip
• Auto-cropped export – the image is trimmed tightly around your signature
• Transparent or white background – transparent PNG for documents, white for
  chat apps and galleries that show transparency as black
• Sharp on every screen – images are exported at your device's resolution
• Save anywhere – pick the folder with the system "Save as" dialog
• Share instantly – send your signature to any app
• English and Indonesian

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account or sign-up. Your signature is created on your device and is only
saved or shared when you choose to.

This app is free and shows ads.
